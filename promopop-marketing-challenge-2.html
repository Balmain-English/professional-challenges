Question 1 • Our Products

Why do hotels and restaurants give away branded items (pens, bags, mugs)? When does a free gift really work?

Think about: useful or not? Memorable? Does the guest keep it or throw it away?

"A good giveaway is useful because..."
"I always keep / throw away free gifts like..."

Question 2 • Our Work

What is more important in our job: a beautiful design, a happy client, or a good price? Can we have all three?

Think about: deadlines, budget, creative freedom, quality, the planet.

"For me, the most important thing is... because..."
"I disagree, because the client..."

🗨️ Useful phrases for team discussion

"I think we should... because..." • "What do you think?" • "I see your point, but..." • "The risk is that..." • "On the other hand..." • "Let's vote." • "Can we find a compromise?"

Welcome to the PromoPop Creative Team!

You work in the marketing and design team at PromoPop Studio. We make branded merchandise (pens, mugs, tote bags, caps, keycard holders, coasters) for hotels, restaurants and cafés. Over the next weeks you will face 12 difficult dilemmas with real clients.

🤝 Client Trust
Is the client happy and loyal?
🎨 Design Quality
Is the final product great?
💰 Profit
Does the company make money?
🌱 Ethics & Planet
Are we honest and responsible?

🎯 How to play

  1. Read the dilemma together. Each role card shows a different point of view.
  2. Discuss for 1-2 minutes (use the timer!). The "Speaks first" role starts.
  3. Choose one answer as a team. Every choice has a good side and a bad side.
  4. See how your 4 meters change. Then answer the discussion question.

There is no perfect answer. The aim is to explain your reasons in English. Click highlighted words like proof to see the meaning.
